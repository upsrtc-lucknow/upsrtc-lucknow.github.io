Privacy Policy

UPSRTC Purchase Orders · last updated 8 October 2026

UPSRTC Purchase Orders (upsrtc-lucknow.github.io) is a web app for preparing purchase orders and monthly purchase statements. This page explains what happens to your data.

Where your data is kept

Everything you enter — purchase orders, vendors, items, settings — is stored only in your own web browser on your own computer. The app has no server and no database of its own; nothing you type is sent to us.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, you sign in with your own Google account and the app:

  • asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it creates itself — it cannot open, read or change any other file in your Drive;
  • creates a folder named “UPSRTC Purchase Orders” in your Drive and saves backup copies of your app data there;
  • reads your Google account’s email address only to show which account is connected;
  • keeps the sign-in token in the browser’s memory only, for about an hour, and never sends it anywhere else.

The backups are in your own Drive, under your control. You can delete them at any time, disconnect from the app, or remove the app’s access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

We do not collect, sell, share or analyse your data. No advertising or tracking is used.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
